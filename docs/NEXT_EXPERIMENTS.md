# Next evidence after v7

V7 implemented explicit boundary learning and observed-only contact/restart proxies. Their measured outcomes are in [BOUNDARY_CAMPAIGN_V7.md](BOUNDARY_CAMPAIGN_V7.md); these are no longer untested proposals. The original v1.5 submission remains deployed. No additional model gain is claimed here.

## Acquire independently labelled dense matches

The largest remaining limitation is fresh synchronized H/A/D and physical-event truth. Reserve complete unseen matches with ordinary play as well as difficult short stops, rapid turnovers and occlusions. Use independently reviewed timestamps with uncertainty intervals; distinguish physical touch, possession acquisition, stop onset and actual restart. Annotators should be blinded to predictions, with disagreement adjudicated. Freeze data identity, split and acceptance criteria before any candidate scoring.

SkillCorner phase labels cannot fill this gap: absent phases remain unknown and may hide disruptions. WCSF and all prior audited matches are already consumed. New corruption variants or directories do not create independent matches. Event-interpolated ball tracks must never be treated as independent observations for event recovery.

## Validate actual contact and restart evidence

The fixed geometric launch audit failed its 90% precision and minimum-support requirements. Do not loosen thresholds against the same references or turn proximity/trajectory change into hard contact labels. An independent video/contact model needs synchronization, calibration, missing-ball and airborne-ball checks, followed by a frozen precision/coverage audit on reviewed annotations. Previous video pilots remain inactive.

## Investigate errors without reusing the final audit

Use exposed development data to compare false splits, missed boundaries, onset/offset timing and short-spell precision/recall. Any new architecture, multiscale branch or segment-consistency loss needs a new preregistered experiment and independent reserved matches. Preserve the incumbent, exact out-of-fold comparisons, half isolation and team symmetry. Promotion requires a measurable gain in complete possession spells and the specific stoppage/short-possession weaknesses, with fixed regression limits; phase accuracy alone is insufficient.
